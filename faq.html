Frequently asked Questions.

How do I join the server?

To join our Minecraft server, simply open Minecraft, click "Multiplayer," then add our server using the IP address provided on the website. Click "Join Ravok Network" to start playing!

What versions of Minecraft are supported?

Our server supports Minecraft versions 1.12.2 through 1.21+. Make sure your game version is compatible before joining. You can find the supported versions on our server page.

Can I use mods or texture packs on the server?

Our server supports certain mods and texture packs, but they must be compatible with the version we’re running. Check the server guidelines for a list of recommended mods and texture packs.

How do I report a player or issue?

If you encounter any problems, such as rule-breaking players or server bugs, you can report them using our in-game reporting system or through our Discord support ticket system.

What are ranks and how do I get them?

Ranks are special titles and perks that you can earn or purchase on our server. Ranks can provide benefits like extra commands, cosmetics, and more. You can view the available ranks and how to obtain them in our store.

How can I support the server?

You can support the server by purchasing ranks, cosmetics, or other items from our store. You can also donate directly through our donation page to help us keep the server running smoothly.
